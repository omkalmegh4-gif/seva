> **SevaSetu (सेवासेतु)** is an ethical cooperative marketplace connecting verified local trade workers (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, technicians, cleaners) with households and businesses. Built on principles of fair compensation, dignity of labor, and worker social security.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **⚖️ Fair Wage Transparency Model**:
  - Eliminates exploitative middleman cuts, ensuring fair pay directly to local artisans and trade workers while maintaining affordable rates for customers.
- **🛡️ Multi-Level Worker Verification**:
  - Stringent identity, trade skill certification, background checks, and community ratings for customer safety and trust.
- **⚡ Rapid Emergency Service Booking**:
  - Dedicated fast-track booking module for urgent household crises (pipe bursts, major electrical faults, security locks).
- **🏥 Worker Welfare & Social Security**:
  - In-app portal dedicated to cooperative medical insurance, micro-pension savings, skill-upgradation workshops, and emergency relief funds.
- **👥 Dual Portals**:
  - **Customer Portal**: Browse vetted workers, book services, track request status, and rate completed work.
  - **Worker Dashboard**: Manage incoming service requests, view transparent earnings, update availability, and track welfare benefits.
- **🌐 Multilingual & Accessible (i18n)**:
  - Built-in internationalization and localization for intuitive use across diverse linguistic communities.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Semantic HTML5
- **Styling**: CSS3 (Modern design tokens, responsive grid, status badges, accessibility considerations)
- **Application Logic**: Vanilla JavaScript (Multi-view routing, state management, emergency booking handler, dynamic translations)
